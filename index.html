Key features

Everything you need to hire locally, safely and fast.

Describe the job, not the worker

Customers explain their need in plain language instead of searching through technical categories. You describe the problem — WorkMatch identifies the solution.

AI matches on five critical factors

Skill, location, budget, availability and ratings are analysed together to produce a shortlist of suitable workers — cutting the time spent browsing and contacting.

Verified workers you can trust

Worker profiles can include identity verification, qualifications, references, customer feedback and a reliability score based on punctuality, communication and completion history.

One platform for local needs

Home services, creative and professional work, technical and business services, and logistics — all in one place, starting with a narrow set of launch categories.

Milestone payments & secure checkout

Larger projects can be divided into milestones. Payment is held securely and released after the customer approves the agreed work — protecting both parties.

Local talent and accountability

The platform prioritises workers in your city or region — supporting faster communication, local knowledge and optional face-to-face collaboration.
